Privacy Policy for PanicNow

Effective date: 14 August 2026

PanicNow is developed and published by Digital Dreams Team.

Information we collect

This version of PanicNow does not create user accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The game stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device. This local information is used only to restore your progress and settings. It is not sent to Digital Dreams Team or any third party.

Advertising and purchases

This version contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Network access and third-party services

PanicNow does not use analytics, advertising networks, or other online tracking services. Google Play may process information independently when you download, update, or review the game. Google's own privacy policy applies to those services.

Children's privacy

PanicNow does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The game's Google Play target-audience selection will be completed before publication.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online services, analytics, advertising, or purchases, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Digital Dreams Team at b4ck2life@gmail.com.
